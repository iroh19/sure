# Research Plan — S.U.R.E. Cross-Layer Deterministic-Override Study

## Research Overview

S.U.R.E. is a deployed edge-AI system that monitors sturgeon welfare and water quality in an indoor fish farm around the clock, because a bad water reading can kill an entire tank within hours. The team behind it never let its AI components have the final word: a small, fully-tested rule checker reviews everything the AI reports and may only raise an alarm the AI missed, never lower one it correctly raised. This paper asks whether that same principle — a simple, checkable, deterministic backstop with escalation-only authority — actually works when applied consistently across every AI-touched decision surface in the system, not just the headline one.

Concretely, the central research question is: in a deployed edge-AI aquaculture welfare monitoring system, does giving deterministic, code-shared logic sole, escalation-only final authority over five architecturally distinct, independently probabilistic surfaces — LLM severity reasoning, LLM-driven agentic tool routing, RAG document retrieval, MLOps retrain triggering, and the edge vision detection pipeline feeding all of these — measurably reduce safety-relevant failure modes relative to trusting each surface's own probabilistic output, and at what documented cost to coverage, recall, or measurement rigor in each layer? The paper is not a claim that the architecture is theoretically novel; it is an evidence-gathering exercise that re-measures, audits, and stress-tests each of the five layers against the live codebase, discloses where the backstop does and does not yet hold (including one deliberate negative result on agentic tool routing), and synthesizes the pattern into a single, honestly-hedged cross-layer claim.

## Goals and Methodology

The plan comprises 11 goals (G1–G11), each targeting one hypothesis or a cross-cutting concern, ordered here roughly by priority and dependency.

**G1 — RAG Retrieval Calibration Reliability (priority 1).** Recovers the full similarity-threshold sweep behind the deployed 0.85 RAG threshold and adds a random/no-retrieval baseline showing the retriever (e5-small) clears chance on the small 8-document/44-chunk corpus. Success: `rag/calibrate.py` reproduces an F1-argmax at 0.84 (vs. deployed 0.85) with a quantified margin above random; fallback is reporting the sweep table and existing corpus-size caveat without a baseline.

**G2 — Eval-Harness / Production Rule-Path Consistency Audit (priority 2).** Confirms `llm-service/eval.py`'s rule computation and `backend/main.py`'s `apply_rule_override` share the same logic, protecting the credibility of the eval/production-drift incident that opens the paper. Success: a documented code-level trace showing no re-implemented severity logic; if divergence is found, it is disclosed as a new drift bug rather than hidden.

**G9 — twin_bridge Mechanism-Evidence Inventory (priority 2, designated fallback goal).** Delivers the achievable evidence path for the digital-twin sub-question using only what already exists: `test_bridge.py`'s unit tests plus a new `FakeTwin`-scripted synthetic edge-case integration test. Success is an honest Results paragraph stating the mechanism is "unit-tested, not exercised against a live twin session" — "validated" is never used absent an actual live run. Requires no missing infrastructure; explicitly the paper's fallback if higher-risk items elsewhere slip.

**G3 — Behavioral Measurement of the Dual-Layer Decision System (priority 3, depends on G2).** Runs AQUA-1B's actual output through `apply_rule_override`, classifies all 8 scenarios into a four-bucket taxonomy (agrees / under-calls-and-escalated / over-calls / unparseable-defaulted-to-ok), runs `test_decision.py` as separate mechanism evidence, and manually codes reasoning text for at least one non-enumerable failure mode. Success ranges from a clean bucket distribution to (minimum viable) reporting a mostly-unparseable output as its own valid finding ("fail-safe is the dominant pathway").

**G4 — Agentic Tool-Routing Negative-Result Robustness (priority 4).** Re-runs `bench_agent.py` to confirm the existing AQUA-1B (0%/0%) and AQUA-7B (60%/50%-constant-answer) results, audits the constant-answer detection logic, and authors ≥4 new scenarios to grow n past 5. Success is full reproduction plus a strengthened or honestly tempered negative result; minimum viable reports the existing n=5 result as a disclosed limitation.

**G5 — Vision Dataset Leakage Audit (priority 5).** A precondition control auditing the full 510-image dataset for clip/video-level leakage beyond the already-caught case, protecting every downstream vision number. Success is a clean audit; if further leakage is found, headline mAP50/precision/recall figures are corrected and caveated.

**G6 — Export-Format Accuracy Loss: Mechanism and Reproducibility (priority 6, depends on G5).** Re-runs the six-configuration export benchmark, repeats CoreML/ANE latency across sessions for variance, and tests the "shared post-processing path" causal claim via a per-image ONNX-vs-TorchScript diff. Success confirms the causal story directly; if the diff shows divergence despite matching aggregate accuracy, the claim is downgraded to "coincidental match, mechanism unconfirmed."

**G7 — Vision Operating-Point Safety Trace (priority 6, depends on G5).** Quantifies the fish-per-frame distribution to state numerically how rare a full-frame miss is under current recall, and sweeps confidence thresholds to confirm the deployed operating point is the true F1-argmax. Success is a falsifiable numeric blind-spot statement; sparse val-set frames make the blind spot read as more urgent, not softened.

**G8 — MLOps PSI Drift-Binning Systematic Sweep (priority 7).** Replaces 4 anecdotal drift-detection points with a full synthetic-degradation sweep comparing quantile-edge vs. equal-width PSI binning, and exercises the retrain gate's three-way decision path against synthetic windows. Success is a smooth-vs-saturating comparison curve plus confirmed gate behavior; historical equal-width figures are cited only if recoverable from git history, else labeled reconstruction.

**G10 — Cross-Layer Thesis Framing & Confirmatory Literature Check (priority 11, depends on G3/G4/G6/G7/G8/G9).** One additional literature search confirms no closer prior instance of the cross-layer claim exists, then the paper's headline framing (the brainstorm-recommended hybrid: incident-as-hook plus hedged cross-layer thesis) is selected and justified, synthesizing all completed layer-level results. If the search surfaces closer prior art, the claim is narrowed rather than retracted.

**G11 — Manuscript-Wide Data-Integrity & Provenance Discipline (priority 12, depends on G1–G9).** A final QA pass: a grep sweep guaranteeing the stale 0.695 recall figure never ships in place of the corrected 0.719, plus a commit-hash/date provenance table for every re-measured number. Minimum viable prioritizes the 0.695 grep check and provenance for the highest-priority numbers if full coverage can't complete before freeze.

## Track Plan

This is an **empirical-only systems paper** — there is no separate theory track. All 11 goals are assigned track "experiment" in `research_goals.json`, following `track_decomposition.json`'s explicit recommendation (`needs_theory_track: false`). The rationale, drawn directly from `brainstorm.json`, is that the paper's only formalization needs — a monotonicity property (`final_severity = max(severity_rule, severity_llm)`), a precise enumerable-vs-non-enumerable failure-mode distinction, the PSI-gating three-way decision rule, and the vision operating-point tradeoff framing — are expository restatements of code that already exists (`backend/main.py`'s `apply_rule_override`, `mlops/drift.py`'s PSI pipeline, `mlops/retrain.py`'s `MIN_IMPROVEMENT` gate), not new mathematical derivations. There is no unresolved convergence, statistical-learning-theory, or optimization question anywhere in the proposal that would justify a dedicated proof track. Spinning one out would, in the brainstorm's own words, "add apparatus without content" — directly contradicting the proposal's Known Limitations warning about unchecked scope growth. Instead, these four formalization items are folded as short Methodology-subsection prose directly into G3 (H1/H2), G7 (H7), and G8 (H5)'s deliverables.

## Timeline and Sequencing

Execution follows the stated priority and dependency ordering. G1 (RAG calibration) and G9 (twin_bridge fallback) run first (priorities 1–2) since both are self-contained and G9 is the designated safety net if later goals slip. G2 (rule-path consistency audit) must complete before G3, since G3's behavioral measurement is only trustworthy once the audit confirms no residual eval/production drift. G4 (agentic tool-routing) proceeds independently in parallel. G5 (vision leakage audit) is a precondition control that must complete before G6 and G7, both of which depend on it. G8 (PSI sweep) proceeds independently. Once the layer-level goals (G3, G4, G6, G7, G8, G9) are complete, G10 synthesizes them into the paper's headline cross-layer framing, confirmed by one final literature check. G11, the manuscript-wide data-integrity pass, runs last, depending on G1 through G9's outputs, immediately before manuscript freeze.

## Risk and Fallback

The single highest infrastructure-risk item in the entire plan is a live Godot/CODESYS digital-twin session (approach TB-3 under G9), which would require a not-yet-existing capture adapter and engine/PLC software that is not currently installed. It is deliberately excluded as a required goal. Instead, **G9 is the paper's designated fallback goal**: it delivers a complete, reviewer-defensible Results subsection using only artifacts already in the repository — `twin_bridge/test_bridge.py`'s existing unit tests plus a new `FakeTwin`-scripted synthetic edge-case integration test — and states the digital twin's status honestly as "unit-tested, not live-validated," with cross-implementation corroboration disclosed as future work. If TB-3's go/no-go checkpoint (one week before manuscript freeze) does produce a live session, that result is reported as an upgrade to G9's baseline, never as a replacement for it. Other goals carry their own pre-committed minimum-viable fallbacks (e.g., G1 reporting the sweep table without a random baseline, G4 disclosing the n=5 negative result as a limitation, G6 downgrading its causal claim to "coincidental match" if the per-image diff doesn't support it), ensuring every goal produces a valid, reportable finding regardless of which branch of its success criteria is reached.
