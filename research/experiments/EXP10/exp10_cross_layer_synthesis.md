## Pattern across all nine

**Zero outright falsifications. Also zero clean, un-nuanced confirmations of the literal hypothesis wording on the layers that mattered most (H1/H2, H5, H7).** Every layer that was actually exercised behaviorally (as opposed to structurally/mechanically) produced at least one honest correction, a numeric resolution, or a genuinely new and previously undocumented finding:

- The two layers confirmed by **structural/mechanism-level** checks alone (EXP02's identity check, EXP06's per-image diff, EXP09's unit-test pass) held up cleanly or *more* strongly than hypothesized.
- The layers that required **exercising actual model/statistical behavior** (EXP03's live AQUA-1B decisions, EXP07's confidence-curve reading, EXP08's PSI sweep) each produced a result that diverges from the hypothesis's literal wording while still supporting — in a more precise, more defensible form — the same underlying design principle (escalate-only override; F1-argmax operating point; drift-sensitive-but-differently-shaped binning fix).

This is exactly the pattern the paper's own pre-committed apparatus (Known Limitations, Risk Assessment row "Running eval.py in model mode... reveals the dynamic rarely or never actually fires") anticipated as a *legitimate* outcome, not a research failure. The synthesis in `results.md` treats it accordingly: as evidence the cross-layer thesis should be stated more precisely, not retracted.
